03Acceptance

Final acceptance

The Client has reviewed the completed website and the deliverables listed in Section 02, and accepts the delivered work as fulfilling the project scope set out in the Engagement Agreement, subject only to any outstanding items recorded in Section 04.

  1. The Client has had the opportunity to review the completed website and its deliverables.
  2. The deliverables listed in Section 02 have been provided.
  3. The website conforms to the agreed project scope, other than any item recorded in Section 04.
  4. The project is accepted as complete.

Acceptance confirms completion of the agreed scope. It does not affect the launch support included in Appendix A of the Engagement Agreement (bug fixes and critical performance issues only).

06Future work

Closeout & future work

Completion of this project closes the agreed project scope. Work beyond that scope, such as new pages or functionality, redesigns, substantial content changes, new integrations or campaign experiences, ongoing development, maintenance, SEO work or strategic digital support, sits outside the completed project unless the Engagement Agreement covers it.

Any such work can be agreed separately, by quotation, retainer or support arrangement. Neither party is obliged to enter into further work.
